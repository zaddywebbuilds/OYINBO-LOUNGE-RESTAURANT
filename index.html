What Oyinbo is

Three rooms, one night

Dinner that tastes like home, a bar that knows what it’s doing, and a floor that fills up by eleven.

Authentic Nigerian kitchen

Slow-cooked stews, fire-grilled suya, jollof that argues for itself. Pepper where pepper belongs.

Craft bar programme

Cocktails built around West African spirits, spices and fruits — plus top-shelf bottles by the pour or the bottle.

Nightlife & live music

Afrobeats, amapiano, R&B and global sounds — live DJs, themed nights and a floor built for everyone.

Our story

Where West African roots meet Toronto nightlife

The name Oyinbo — used across West Africa for someone from another world — signals exactly who this place is for: everyone, from every background.

Founded by Mola Stanley Ilesanmi, the lounge brings the confidence of Lagos hospitality to North York: a kitchen that doesn’t water anything down, a bar that stays open late, and a room where the music carries the night.
