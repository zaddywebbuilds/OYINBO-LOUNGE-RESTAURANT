The week

What’s on, and when

Doors follow our kitchen hours. Thursday through Saturday we run until 3am.

Friday

Friday Vibes with DJ Rolland

The week lands. Afrobeats into the early hours, kitchen running alongside the bar until 3am. Come for suya, stay for the floor.

Saturday

Afrobeats & Amapiano

The big one. Full floor, live DJ, doors until 3am. Call ahead if you’re bringing a group — Saturday fills fast.

Sunday

Live Highlife

Slower, warmer, grown. We open at 5pm and run to 1am — the right night for a long table and a longer conversation.

Mon – Thu

Kitchen & bar

Open from midday. Quieter rooms, same kitchen. Wednesday runs to 2am and Thursday to 3am when the weekend starts early.
