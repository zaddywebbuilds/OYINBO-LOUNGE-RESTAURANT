Our story

Where West African roots meet Toronto nightlife

The name Oyinbo — used across West Africa for someone from another world — signals exactly who this place is for: everyone, from every background.

Founded by Mola Stanley Ilesanmi, the lounge brings the confidence of Lagos hospitality to North York: a kitchen that doesn’t water anything down, a bar that stays open late, and a room where the music carries the night.

Nightlife

There’s always something happening

Afrobeats, amapiano, R&B and global sounds. Live DJs, themed nights and a floor built for everyone.

Friday Vibes with DJ Rolland

The week lands. Afrobeats into the early hours, kitchen running alongside the bar until 3am.

Afrobeats & Amapiano Saturdays

The big one. Full floor, live DJ, doors until 3am. Call ahead if you’re bringing a group.

Live Highlife Sundays

Slower, warmer, grown. We open at 5pm and run to 1am — the night for a long table.

Private bookings & Birthday Club

Your night, your room

Birthdays, send-offs, work parties. Call us and we’ll put the room together around what you need. Join the Birthday Club and the kitchen sends out a complimentary dessert and a glass of palm wine on your birthday — every year.
